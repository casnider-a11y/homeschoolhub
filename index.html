Understanding your aptitudes

Aptitudes are natural talents — not the same as knowledge or grades. There are no good or bad scores. High scores point to outlets worth seeking. Lower scores suggest a different approach or helpful systems (and can even be an advantage). Hands-on dexterity tests are in-person add-ons, coming later.
